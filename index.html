How to use DwwTik

Use the tool to submit a link to a supported TikTok or Instagram post. The steps are simple:

  1. Open the TikTok or Instagram post you want to access and copy its link.
  2. Select the matching platform above, then paste the link into the input field.
  3. Select Find video. If the service returns a downloadable video, use the download option shown on the page.

Only download content you have permission to save. Respect creators’ rights and the platform’s terms. DwwTik is an independent tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by TikTok or Instagram.

Supported links and limitations

DwwTik currently offers link processing for supported TikTok and Instagram video posts. A link may not work if the post is private, removed, region-restricted, unavailable to the processing service, or otherwise unsupported. Availability can also change when a third-party service changes its systems or limits.

DwwTik cannot guarantee that every link will be processed, that every video quality will be available, or that a download will always succeed.

Troubleshooting

Check the link

Copy the link again from the original post and make sure you selected the matching platform. Remove any extra text before or after the URL.

If the video cannot be processed

Confirm that the post is still available and accessible to you. Private, deleted, restricted, or unsupported posts may not work. You can try again later if the service is temporarily unavailable or has reached a request limit.

If the download does not start

Check your browser’s download prompt and connection. If it still fails, reload the page and try once more. Repeated attempts may not help when the external service is unavailable.

Frequently asked questions

How do I try downloading a TikTok video?

Copy the TikTok post link, select TikTok in DwwTik, paste the link, and select Find video. If a downloadable file is returned, choose the download option.

Can I download any Instagram video?

No. Only supported links may work. Private, removed, restricted, or unsupported posts might not be available to the tool.

Can I download a private video?

DwwTik is not designed to bypass privacy settings or access controls. Only use links and content you are authorized to access.

Why did my link fail?

The link may be incorrect, the post may be unavailable, or the external processing service may be temporarily limited or unable to handle that post. Check the URL and try again later.

Does DwwTik guarantee watermark-free downloads?

No. Available file options depend on what the processing service returns for a particular post. DwwTik cannot guarantee that a watermark-free file will be available.

Does DwwTik store my video or link?

The link you submit is sent to the site’s processing service so it can attempt to retrieve video information. The site may also rely on hosting and third-party providers that process technical request data. See the Privacy Policy for more information; do not submit sensitive or private links.

About DwwTik

DwwTik is a simple web tool for trying to process supported TikTok and Instagram video links in one place. It provides a link input, platform selection, and download options when a compatible result is available. It does not host a catalogue of posts, and successful processing is not guaranteed.

Use the tool responsibly and make sure you have the necessary rights or permission before saving or sharing someone else’s content.
